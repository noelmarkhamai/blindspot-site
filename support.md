# Blindspot support

Blindspot shows your poker tournament stack in big blinds on your iPhone, Lock Screen, Dynamic Island and Apple Watch.

## Common questions
**The Live Activity has gone.** iOS only lets an app start a Live Activity while the app is open. Open Blindspot and make any change, and it starts again.

**Clock alerts don't arrive.** Check that notifications are allowed for Blindspot in Settings → Notifications, and that the alert is switched on in Blindspot → Settings → Clock alerts.

## Contact
Email [blindspot@markham.io](mailto:blindspot@markham.io) with your iPhone and Watch models, and the version shown in Blindspot → Settings → About.
